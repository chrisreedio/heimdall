Dangling records

{{if .DangZones}}

A and AAAA records whose address is none of your hosts, not Cloudflare's, not private and not labelled. The hub tries a plain TCP connect to each address on 443 and 80 (nothing is sent). Answers: a server you don't monitor; if it's yours, add or label it, if not, someone else may be serving your name. No answer: likely a leftover, safe to delete. A hub whose outbound traffic is firewalled sees no answer everywhere. Only admins see this list.
